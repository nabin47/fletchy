## Target audience and content

- Target age groups: **13-15, 16-17, 18 and over**. Do **not** select any group under 13. Fletchy is a general-audience puzzle game, not designed for children, and selecting under-13 groups brings in the Families policy requirements.
- "Could your store listing unintentionally appeal to children?" **No.** The art is abstract arrows on a grid, with no characters and no child-directed wording.

## Data safety

- Does your app collect or share any of the required user data types? **No.**
- Result: the store listing shows **"No data collected"** and **"No data shared with third parties"**.
- Why this is accurate:
  - The app has no account and no server. It makes no network requests (the Android build has no INTERNET permission) and contains no third-party SDKs.
  - Progress (scores, Daily results, streak, settings) is stored only on the device, in app storage. On-device storage is not "collection" in Play's definition.
  - Sharing a Daily result is user-initiated, through the Android share sheet. The app itself sends nothing.
- Data deletion: not applicable (no data collected). Uninstalling the app removes all local progress.
